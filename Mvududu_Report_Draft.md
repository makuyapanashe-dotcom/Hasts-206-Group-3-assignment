HASTS 206 Group 3 Assignment - Report Draft
Author:Mutsawashe Mvududu (Survey Lead-in place of Leona)

1. Objectives
To determine what proportion of Zimbabwean households use clean cooking fuel (defined as electricity, LPG gas, or solar) as their main cooking fuel.
To determine what proportion of solid-fuel users (households using firewood or charcoal) own an improved cookstove.
To calculate the mean monthly household spending on cooking fuel in USD.



2. Recommendations
Add a secondary cooking-fuel item and a fuel-stacking frequency question to the questionnaire, and collect a listing file with household status. 
Standardise the expenditure probe and make the "zero" option mandatory in the KoBo form.

 Enumerator Field Report
Author: Pride Neghanda (Enumerator 3)

Fieldwork Summary
 Conducted pilot interviews for the group.
 Total interviews conducted: 34
 Location: Urban and Rural 

Observations
 The data collection process was generally successful, although some respondents experienced difficulties recalling their monthly cooking fuel expenditure, making probing necessary to obtain accurate responses
 The digital Kobo toolbox form performed well, with its validation rules and skip patterns functioning correctly and allowing successful submissions of pilot responses
